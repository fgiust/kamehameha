～間に

# You can use the pattern "間に" when a certain event B takes place in the middle of another event, A.
# Most often, event A is described with ている if the verb takes ている for an action in progress.
# The verb for A is in the present tense, even when clause A describes a situation in the past.

While my mother is cooking dinner, I set the table.
Mentre mia madre cucina la cena, io apparecchio la tavola.
{私[わたし]は|}母[はは]が{晩[ばん]御[ご]飯[はん]|晩[ばん]ご飯[はん]|夕食[ゆうしょく]|夕飯[ゆうはん]}を作[つく]っている間に、テーブルをセットします

While the children are playing in the park, I read a book on the bench.
Mentre i bambini giocano nel parco, io leggo un libro sulla panchina.
{私[わたし]は|}子供[こども]たちが公園[こうえん]で遊[あそ]んでいる間に、ベンチで本[ほん]を読[よ]みます

While my father is driving, I listen to music in the car.
Mentre mio padre guida, io ascolto la musica in macchina.
{私[わたし]は|}父[ちち]が運転[うんてん]している間に、車[くるま]の中[なか]で音楽[おんがく]を聞[き]きます

While the teacher is writing on the blackboard, the students are chatting.
Mentre l'insegnante scrive sulla lavagna, gli studenti stanno chiacchierando.
先生[せんせい]が黒板[こくばん]に書[か]いている間に、生徒[せいと]たちがおしゃべりしています

While my roommate is sleeping, I study in the library.
Mentre il mio coinquilino dorme, io studio in biblioteca.
{私[わたし]は|}ルームメートが寝[ね]ている間に、図書館[としょかん]で勉強[べんきょう]します

While Yamashita-san is practicing the guitar, I make tea.
Mentre la signora Yamashita si esercita con la chitarra, io preparo il tè.
{私[わたし]は|}山下さんがギターを練習[れんしゅう]している間に、お茶[ちゃ]を入[い]れます

While the baby is taking a nap, I do the cleaning.
Mentre il bebè fa il pisolino, io faccio le pulizie.
{私[わたし]は|}赤[あか]ちゃんが昼寝[ひるね]をしている間に、掃除[そうじ]をします
